Why this film, why now

“Go to college, get a good job, and you’ll have an easy life.” What if that was the wrong goal?

I got my job in the information economy, then watched as hundreds, then thousands, and now tens of thousands of people were laid off.

Meanwhile, people are retiring out of the trades faster than we’re replacing them. There’s a boom in demand we can’t meet, because there aren’t enough skilled workers.

A nation of too many thinkers and not enough doers leaves all of us vulnerable: to layoffs, to crowded job markets, and to breakdowns in our homes and our infrastructure. The people who understand that gap are the ones who will build successful lives as the economy shifts.

That’s why I wrote The Trade Gap. Books are great, but we live on our screens. To reach students, parents and a workforce ready to retrain, we need to tell this story as a film, and we need partners in the trades to help tell it.

Zachary HansonAuthor, The Trade Gap
